# Flood-Buffer-Capacity
Calculation of Flood Buffer Capacity of Seoul's 20 selected waterfronts

This was used for my Master Thesis called "Shape of Water and Daily Life: Measuring and Leveraging the Climate-Adaptive Potentials of Seoul's Waterfronts" (https://resolver.tudelft.nl/uuid:7aa7a4f9-82fa-4bdc-a00e-a845fbc49763)
It assumes that a severe rainfall occurs simulataneously in the catchment basins of the selected waterways (Hangang river, Hongje, Jungrang, Anyang, Tan) and calculates how well waterfronts can withstand overflow.
Only spatial factors were considered in the calculation - size of catchment basins, runoff coefficients according to land use, shape and size of waterfronts.

The rainfall scenario used for calculation was based on 
Yoon, S.-K., & Choi, H. (2019). 서울시 기후변화 영향을 고려한 확률강우량 산정 [Estimation of the future probable precipitation in accordance with the changing climate in Seoul]. Seoul Institute of Technology.
Runoff coefficients are median values of ranges used by the Ministry of Land, Infrastructure and Transportation to design drainage systems, based on
한국수자원학회 [Korean Water Resources Association] & 한국하천협회 [Korea River Association]. (2019). 하천설계기준해설(2019) [Commentary on River Design Standards (2019)]. 한국수자원학회 [Korean Water Resources Association].
